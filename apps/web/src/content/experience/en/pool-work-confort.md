I worked on three services of the Pool Work Confort platform: the website and online store, an email microservice and an internal management system.

- **Website and online store:** redesign, SEO optimization, stronger security and UX improvements, built with Nuxt and PHP. It includes a cache-optimized exchange rate integration and integrates with the email microservice.
- **Email microservice:** handles every email the platform sends, removing the dependency on third-party email providers. Node.js, TypeScript, SMTP and Cloudflare Workers.
- **Commercial and operations management system:** automates quotes, sales tracking and inventory, and keeps products in sync with the online store. Node.js, TypeScript, Docker and Astro, integrated with the email microservice.
