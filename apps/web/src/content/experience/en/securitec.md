I joined in 2019 as a Full Stack Engineer. In March 2020 I proposed how to implement and develop Ticker, defined its architecture and went on to lead the team that built it, 4 to 5 people.

- Ticker is a customer service platform where every channel lands in a single inbox, in real time. It has been in production for 5 years with B2B customers in retail and services.
- Messages arrive instantly even when the backend runs on several instances: WebSockets and Redis.
- I connected WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, SMS and a payment gateway.
- Backend in NestJS and MongoDB, on Huawei Cloud and GCP. I also built the corporate site’s backend, in PHP.
